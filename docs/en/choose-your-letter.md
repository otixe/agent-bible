# Choose your letter

| | [Single agent](../../carta/en/LETTER-SOLO-AGENT.md) | [Team lead](../../carta/en/LETTER-TEAM-LEAD.md) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Use it for** | One agent that works for one person. | An agent that will coordinate other agents. |
| **Harness inside** | The short one: cycle, risk classes, verify the effect. | The full office Harness: roles, executor/QA separation, a task board, proportionate contracts, delegation. |
| **First-run questions** | The four numbered ones (see below). | The four, **plus** a fifth about the team: who is on it, roles and permissions, QA and audit cover, how they communicate. |
| **Length** | ≈ 8,500–9,000 characters | ≈ 13,200–13,500 characters |
| **Typical platforms** | Any chat or agent platform. | Platforms where several agents can exist and exchange work. |

## The first-run questions

Both letters make the agent's first reply consist of these questions and nothing else:

1. **My name and who I am.** What it should be called and who you want it to be.
2. **My responsible human.** Your name or alias, how to address you, your time zone and your boundaries.
3. **My work.** Its role in three levels — purpose, functions, and limits (what it must *not* do).
4. **Tools, permissions and memory.** What it may really use, what needs your confirmation, how its memory works — and where `SOUL.md` should live.

## Not sure?

Start with the **single agent**. You can move to the team-lead letter later: the SOUL the agent wrote is plain text and carries over. A lead for a one-person team adds ceremony you do not need.

## Size matters on some platforms

Each letter is self-contained, so it is long. Some platforms cap how much text an instruction field accepts. If yours does, the [platform page](platforms/README.md) shows the fallback: a short pointer in the instructions and the full letter as an attached file, or the letter as the first message of a conversation.
